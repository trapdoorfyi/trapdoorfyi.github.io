Typed your card into a toll-text page?

Do this now. One page, free, no email needed. It goes with the Trapdoor video "The Unpaid Toll Text Is a Factory".

Download the checklist (PDF)

In this order. The first two matter most, because the scam's real goal is getting your card into the criminal's phone wallet.

  1. Call your bank on the number on the back of the card. Say you entered it on a scam site. Ask them to lock it and issue a new number. Don't use any number from the text.
  2. Ask the bank to remove every Apple Pay or Google Wallet copy of the card you don't recognize. Deleting the card from your own phone doesn't remove it from theirs.
  3. Never read out or type a one-time code you didn't ask for. Read verification texts to the end: a code "for a purchase" may say it's for adding your card to a new device.
  4. Go through recent transactions and dispute anything you didn't make. Small test charges come first. Check again over the next few weeks.
  5. Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and the FBI's ic3.gov, and forward the text to 7726 (SPAM). Then delete it.
  6. Gave more than a card (your Social Security number, a bank login, a password)? Follow the recovery plan at IdentityTheft.gov and change any reused passwords.
